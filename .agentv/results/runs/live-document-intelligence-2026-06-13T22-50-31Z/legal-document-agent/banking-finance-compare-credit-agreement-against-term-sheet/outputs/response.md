## Prioritized deviation report: draft credit agreement vs. executed term sheet

Below is a borrower-side comparison of the draft credit agreement against the executed term sheet, prioritized by likely economic and control impact. Citations reference the supplied filenames and locators.

### 1) SOFR floor increased; revolver pricing grid appears less favorable
**Severity:** High  
**Borrower impact:** Negative / lender-favorable

**Deviation**
- The executed term sheet sets Term SOFR floor at **0.75%** for Term Loan A and DDTL, with revolver pricing stepping down to **SOFR + 3.50%** and **SOFR + 3.25%** at lower leverage levels.  
  - Source: `documents/executed-term-sheet.docx`, Section 4.1; Section 4.2; Exhibit C
- The draft sets a **1.00% SOFR floor** and shows only **two revolver tiers**: **3.75%** above **3.50:1.00 leverage** and **3.50%** below that.  
  - Source: `documents/draft-credit-agreement.docx`, Section 2.08(a); Section 1.01 definition of SOFR

**Why it matters**
- The higher SOFR floor is economically adverse even if headline margin is unchanged.
- The apparent omission of the third, more favorable revolver tier may reduce pricing benefits at lower leverage.

**Borrower recommendation**
- Restore the **0.75% SOFR floor**.
- Confirm whether the missing **3.25% revolver tier** was intended; if not, reinstate the full term-sheet grid.
